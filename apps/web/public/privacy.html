Privacy policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

Brainrotten (the website and the BrainRotGram app) turns topics and coding sessions into short learning videos.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture through Amazon Cognito. We store the topics, scripts and learning sessions you or your coding agent submit, the videos made from them, and your progress (watch position, quiz answers, saved videos).

How we use it

Only to run the service: to sign you in, make and show your videos, keep your progress and enforce the free video limit. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Where it is processed

Data is stored on Amazon Web Services in the Mumbai region (ap-south-1). To make a video, the script text is sent to our speech provider (ElevenLabs) and, when you give a topic instead of a script, to an AI script provider. Videos and their files are deleted automatically after 30 days.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time. To delete your account and data, contact us through the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen.
